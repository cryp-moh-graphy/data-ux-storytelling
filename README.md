### Mathematical Modeling of Generational Assimilation
**Categories:** Mathematical Modeling — Market & Behavioral Research · Strategic Communication  
**Focus:** Interdisciplinary analysis · Systems thinking · Mathematical decay frameworks  

Modeled cultural assimilation as a process of exponential dilution across generations — approximated first by $y = (1/2)^n$, then generalized to $y = (1/k)^n$ as the number of competing cultural inputs grows, and further extended to continuous decay $A(t) = A_0 e^{-\lambda t}$. Applied these frameworks to Bernardine Evaristo's *Girl, Woman, Other* to argue that assimilation is a recursive, multi-directional redistribution of identity rather than a linear replacement — demonstrated through Carole's linguistic shift, Yazz's categorical rejection, and Bummi's environmental softening over time.

### Blockchain Payment Security: Address Generation and Risk Analysis
**Categories:** Technical Writing · Compliance & Risk Communication  
**Focus:** Systems analysis · Cryptographic risk assessment · Technical communication  

Analyzed how Bitcoin and Ethereum derive wallet addresses — SHA-256 -> RIPEMD-160 -> Base58Check for Bitcoin; Keccak-256 truncated to 20 bytes -> EIP-55 checksum for Ethereum — and abstracted both as a unidirectional pipeline: Private Key -> Public Key -> Hashed Address. Evaluated operational risk at each transformation layer: key custody failure, stale-address misdirection, and the compliance implications of transaction irreversibility in financial oversight contexts.

### Comparative Analysis of Generational Behavioral Frameworks
**Categories:** Behavioral Analysis — Market Research · Behavioral Research · UX Research  
**Focus:** Behavioral analysis · Framework application · Perception modeling  

Constructed a three-variable perceptual model — structural (era, socioeconomic status, access), social (family environment, peer influence, cultural norms), and psychological (motivation, expectations, prior experience) — and applied it to analyze how Amma, Yazz, and Dominique in *Girl, Woman, Other* each operationalize feminism differently from the same textual world. Demonstrated that a single data point carries no fixed meaning outside an observer's internal benchmarks — the same framework applicable to interpreting user behavior, survey data, or A/B test results.
